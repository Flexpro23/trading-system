Accounts

Each account has its own paper balance and its own free margin; a trade is sized from, and limited by, the account that places it. Changing a starting balance moves that account's balance by the same amount and keeps its trade history. The swing bot's starting balance is set in user_data/config.json and needs a bot restart. Your quick desk tests count in your account. Daily limits still apply to all accounts together.

Every position

A position is one entry; its take-profit pieces and partial closes count inside it. Win rate counts closed positions. Fees are 0.05% a side on the desk's own trades and the bot's recorded fees on its trades. Notes are measurements of past trades, not advice on what to trade.

Strategy lab

Loading

One paper profile per strategy, each with its own 1,000 USDT. Lab bots risk a fixed 1% per trade; the swing bot and the scalp bot use their desk settings. R is profit divided by what the trade risked at its first stop: +1 R means it made what it risked. The new lab bots are outside the desk's account total and daily limits; the swing bot and the scalp bot are desk accounts and stay inside them. Paper only.
